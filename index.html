The Great Larp is a fictional global conflict in the setting of the Great Fandom Wars. The conflict is depicted as a war between an extraordinarily large coalition of fictional universes, fandoms, internet communities, video games, cartoons, films, companies, countries, memes, music genres and online platforms, collectively known as the Allies, and a single opposing faction known as Lesbian Space princess.

The conflict is presented as occurring across Earth, fictional universes, video games, social media, television networks, outer space and numerous fictional dimensions. According to fictional Allied records, the war began in the Year of the First Larp and ended six years later with the collapse of Lesbian Space princess. The exact chronology varies between sources because several calendars were used simultaneously by different fandoms.

The Great Larp is entirely fictional and is intended as a parody of conventional historical accounts of major twentieth-century conflicts. None of the events, statistics, military organizations or historical claims described below are real.

Contents

    Background

    Before the Great Larp, fictional universes generally existed independently. Characters from different franchises occasionally crossed dimensions, collaborated or fought one another, but no permanent military organization existed that could coordinate them on a global scale.

    The situation changed following the discovery of the Larp Network, an alleged interdimensional communication system capable of connecting fictional universes, video games, social media platforms and human communities. The discovery produced an unprecedented increase in crossover activity.

    The first major incident occurred when an unidentified signal appeared simultaneously on YouTube, TikTok, Roblox, Minecraft, television broadcasts and several fictional universes. The signal contained only the phrase “the larp has begun”.

    The Allied Fandom Historical Commission later identified this event as the Beginning of the Larp.

    Origins of the Conflict

    The precise causes of the conflict remain disputed by fictional historians.

    The Allied Fandom Historical Commission attributes the war primarily to territorial disputes involving fictional dimensions and disagreements regarding control of the Larp Network. The Internet War Records instead emphasize a series of meme-related incidents.

    Lesbian Space princess sources, where available, claim that the Allies had created an excessively large military coalition and were attempting to impose a standardized fandom culture on independent communities.

    The immediate cause of the war was the disappearance of the Great Server Key, an object allegedly capable of granting control over every connected fictional server. Both sides accused the other of stealing the key. Neither side ever conclusively proved that the key had existed.

    Declaration of War

    The war officially began following the Space Princess Ultimatum. Lesbian Space princess demanded that the Allies surrender the Great Server Key and recognize the sovereignty of the Space Princess Territories.

    The Allies rejected the ultimatum.

    The following morning, a Roblox server containing 47,000 fictional military units was allegedly attacked. The incident became known as the Roblox Server Incident and was subsequently used by both sides as justification for full-scale war.

    The Allied Council issued the Declaration of the Great Larp shortly afterward.

    Major Battles

    Battle of the Infinite Lobby

    The first major engagement occurred inside an enormous multiplayer lobby. The Allied forces entered with approximately 4.8 billion fictional units. The opposing force reportedly consisted of seven Space Princess soldiers and an unknown number of automated defense systems. The battle lasted approximately 11 minutes. The Allies declared victory after gaining control of the lobby.

    Battle of Skibidi City

    The Battle of Skibidi City became one of the largest urban engagements of the war. Skibidi Toilet units initially controlled the city. Allied forces from Minecraft, Call of Duty, Fortnite, FNaF and several superhero factions launched a coordinated attack. The battle ended after a giant fictional toilet was mistakenly classified as an allied artillery platform.

    Battle of Dragon Ball Sector

    The Dragon Ball Sector became a major turning point. Dragon Ball forces demonstrated overwhelming offensive capabilities. According to fictional military records, one unidentified combatant destroyed 14,000 enemy bases before asking where the nearest restaurant was.

    Battle of the Digital Circus

    The Digital Circus Theater was characterized by rapidly changing terrain, unreliable physics and repeated reality resets. Several enemy units became trapped inside an infinite loading screen.

    Battle of Godzilla Bay

    Godzilla participated directly in the Battle of Godzilla Bay. Allied naval forces reportedly requested that Godzilla avoid damaging friendly ships. Godzilla ignored the request. Despite the resulting destruction, the operation was classified as an Allied victory.

    Battle of the Minecraft Overworld

    The Minecraft Theater became a major logistical center. The Allies constructed an estimated 900 billion blocks of defensive infrastructure. Much of the infrastructure was destroyed within minutes by unidentified entities carrying beds.

    Battle of the Fandom Nexus

    The Fandom Nexus was the largest crossover battle of the conflict. Nearly every major Allied faction participated. The battle lasted 63 days according to Allied records, although several participants claimed that the battle lasted only 12 minutes because of repeated timeline resets.

    Technology and Weapons

    Larp Engine

    The Larp Engine was the Allies' principal strategic technology. The machine allowed units from incompatible universes to operate under the same fictional physics. Its main disadvantage was that nobody knew how to turn it off.

    Meme Bomb

    The Meme Bomb was an information weapon designed to spread a single meme across every connected platform. The largest recorded deployment reached 92% of all active fictional Internet users.

    Respawn Technology

    Gaming factions introduced respawn technology. This significantly complicated casualty calculations. A soldier could theoretically be killed hundreds of times during a single battle and return immediately.

    Propaganda and Memes

    Propaganda played an important role in the conflict. The Allies established the Allied Meme Bureau, which produced posters, videos, songs and memes intended to maintain morale.

    Common Allied slogans included “One fandom, one server”, “Keep the lobby alive”, “Victory at 60 FPS” and “Never surrender the Great Server Key”.

    Lesbian Space princess developed its own propaganda system, centered on the claim that the Allied Coalition was too large to possess a coherent political ideology.

    The Internet Front

    The Internet Front was one of the most active theaters. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts became major information centers.

    The Gaming Front

    The Gaming Front included nearly every major gaming community. Minecraft served as the principal engineering center. Roblox became the recruitment network. Fortnite provided rapid deployment infrastructure. Call of Duty and Counter-Strike supplied conventional tactical units. DOOM supplied elite assault forces. Among Us provided intelligence personnel, although their reliability was frequently questioned.

    FNaF units were primarily deployed at night. Dead Rails supplied railway logistics. R.E.P.O. units were assigned to resource recovery. Balatro was classified as a strategic gambling-adjacent logistics system.

    The Fandom Front

    The Fandom Front consisted of fictional universes whose populations had previously remained isolated. The war caused unprecedented levels of crossover. Anime characters fought cartoon characters. Superheroes fought monsters. Video game characters fought fictional soldiers. Internet personalities were placed in command of military units.

    Turning Points

    Historians generally identify five major turning points: the Battle of Godzilla Bay, the Dragon Ball Sector Offensive, the Battle of the Fandom Nexus, Operation Upload, and the Battle of the Infinite Server.

    After the Battle of the Infinite Server, Lesbian Space princess lost control of its primary communications network.

    Collapse of Lesbian Space princess

    By the sixth year of the war, Lesbian Space princess faced severe logistical problems. The faction had lost most of its servers, several dimensions and approximately 82% of its available dramatic entrances.

    The final collapse occurred during the Battle of Princessian Prime. Allied forces surrounded the capital while Internet forces disrupted communications. The Space Princess ordered a final counterattack. The attack failed because 94% of the troops were accidentally sent to a Minecraft server.

    The remaining forces surrendered after the Allied Coalition offered unlimited access to the post-war meme archive.

    Aftermath

    The end of the war created a new political order known as the Post-Larp Order. The Allied Coalition was reorganized into the United Fandom Assembly.

    The organization attempted to prevent future conflicts by creating standardized rules for crossovers. The first rule prohibited destroying entire universes without prior authorization. The second required participants to agree on power scaling before battles. The third required all major characters to attend diplomatic meetings. The third rule was immediately abandoned.

    Great Reconstruction

    LEGO was given responsibility for rebuilding physical infrastructure. Minecraft handled block-based territories. Roblox rebuilt damaged servers. SCP Foundation personnel contained damaged anomalies. YouTube and TikTok restored damaged information networks. Coca-Cola and Pepsi jointly rebuilt several vending-machine networks after the Great Vending Machine Crisis.

    Legacy

    The Great Larp became one of the most important events in fictional Internet history. The conflict permanently changed relationships between fandoms. Crossovers became more common and gaming communities developed new forms of cooperative storytelling.

    Meme warfare became recognized as a legitimate fictional military discipline. The war also created a new academic field known as Larpology.

    The University of the Internet created the first Chair of Meme History. The department's first professor resigned after three days because students kept posting reaction images during lectures.
